The `DECOUVERTE` promotion is restricted to subscription checkout sessions. Credit packs and SnapRouge payments must keep their regular prices.

**Why:** The campaign is intended to acquire subscribers without reducing the price of one-time purchases or the SnapRouge product.

**How to apply:** Keep the code validated server-side before passing the Stripe promotion code to Checkout, and keep the discounted subscription amounts visible in the pricing UI.
