---
name: Landing examples restraint
description: The examples section should use restrained visual treatment rather than multiple simultaneous decorative effects.
---

For the landing examples section, prioritize clear before/after imagery with minimal framing and only a few supporting accents.

**Why:** A previous treatment with rotations, large halos, oversized arrows, and decorative labels made the section feel visually overloaded.

**How to apply:** Keep cards straight, use a subtle container, a small transformation cue, and restrained blue emphasis on the result.
